How I operate

The working style behind the numbers

My instinct is structure-first. I assume patterns in data are rarely just about individual cases. More often, they tell us something about systems, incentives, bottlenecks, or blind spots.

That matters in corporate settings just as much as in public systems. A backlog, a drop in trust, a decline in repeat customers, or a branch drifting off target are not just isolated events. They are signals. The task is to read them early, before they deepen into larger operational or strategic problems.
