Case 04 · Retail performance intelligence

Can strong sales hide a struggling branch?

Sales figures alone cannot reveal whether a branch is difficult to use, understaffed or repeatedly out of stock. This scenario examines how comparing revenue with customer and operational indicators changes the interpretation of apparent performance.

Reading the evidence

What the comparison can—and cannot—tell us.

A branch with high revenue and low satisfaction could be trading on location or customer necessity, whereas low revenue with low friction could indicate unrealised growth potential. Both require investigation before any recommendation.

Limit of inference: Scores are illustrative 0–100 indices with no defined commercial scale, timestamps, sampling basis, operational denominators or audited financial inputs. A high score for queue or staffing pressure indicates greater strain, unlike scores for satisfaction and reliability. No profitability or impact estimate is claimed.
